saiscase study

A redesign concept for the website of a historic Sicilian bus company: from a dated, confusing site to a clear experience built around booking, information and self-service, on the website and in a new mobile app.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Project
Website & app redesign (concept)
Platform
Desktop website & iOS app
Deliverables
Audit, benchmark, UI design, prototypes

01 — The problem

A site that gets in the way

The current website of SAIS Trasporti is dated and confusing: information is scattered, the hierarchy is unclear and many of the services travellers expect today are hard to find or missing. Buying a ticket, finding a ticket office or getting help should take seconds, not a hunt through the menus.

Outdated interface

A visual language that no longer reflects a company travelling since 1922, and that makes the service feel less reliable than it is.

Scattered information

Timetables, offers, ticket offices and contacts live in different places, with no clear path between them.

Booking is not the priority

The main action, searching and buying a ticket, doesn’t stand out from the rest of the content.

Methodology

Step 1

Site audit

An analysis of how the current site is built: pages, contents and paths, to understand what works and what doesn’t.

Step 2

Benchmark

A study of Italian transport companies, by rail, coach and city transit, to see how they handle booking, information and support.

Step 3

Prioritisation

Deciding what to keep, what to cut and what to add, then organising it into a clearer structure.

Step 4

UI design

The new desktop site, designed page by page with a consistent system of colours, cards and components.

02 — Research

What the best already do well

Five Italian transport companies were analysed, across rail, long-distance coaches and city transport: Trenitalia, Trenord, FlixBus, Itabus and ATM Milano. What they do well came down to six patterns, each one a feature the new SAIS site brings in.

Booking comes first

Every company opens with the trip search, the one thing almost everyone comes for.

  • Trenitalia
  • FlixBus
  • Itabus

A ticket, without logging in

Trenitalia lets travellers find and change a ticket with PNR and email, FlixBus with the booking number.

  • Trenitalia
  • FlixBus

Live information

Real-time train progress, bus tracking and waiting times, plus alerts before departure.

  • Trenord
  • FlixBus
  • ATM

A personal space

“I miei viaggi”, “I miei biglietti”: trips, tickets and subscriptions in one place, and the app becomes the ticket.

  • Trenitalia
  • Trenord
  • Itabus

Offers you can find

Promotions have their own space and can be applied straight to new trips.

  • Itabus

Offices with their hours

ATM lists every ATM Point with its opening hours, so nobody walks to a closed office.

  • ATM Milano

04 — The website

The new site, page by page

Before & after

Two of the pages that changed the most, side by side: the current website and the redesign.

Home

Ticket offices

The pages

Home

The search widget comes first: departure, arrival, dates and passengers in a single block. A second tab lets travellers find a ticket with email and PNR or track their bus.

Below, the main destinations with travel time and lowest fare, the services on board, the app, the offers and the help paths.

Destination page

Every city has its own page, with the bus stop on the map and the services nearby: terminal, parking, connections and accessibility.

Next to it, a booking form with the destination already filled in, and more destinations to keep exploring.

Ticket offices

All the ticket offices in one grid, with a search bar to find the nearest one. Each card shows opening hours, phone and address.

The pin icon opens the card with the map, and a banner keeps customer support one click away.

Offers

Every fare has its own page, explaining what it is, how it works and its conditions, like You & Me, the discounted fare for two passengers.

At the bottom, the active routes with the fare for two, ready to book.

Contacts

Three clear paths, customer care, complaints and FAQ, so everyone gets to the right answer without guessing.

Call centre, email and head office with their hours, a complete complaint form answered within 24 hours, and the most common questions in an accordion.

Dashboard

The personal area puts the essentials in one place: trips taken, upcoming departures, loyalty points and active tickets.

Each upcoming trip has its ticket one tap away, and Club SAIS turns kilometres into free tickets.

05 — The app

The same journey, in your pocket

The website is only half of the journey. The SAIS app uses the same design system, shaped for what people do on the move: buying in a few taps, keeping the ticket in their pocket and following the bus live.

Buy a ticket in a few taps

Search, compare the runs of the day and pick your seat on the bus map. Every step keeps the trip in view, so you always know what you are paying for.

The ticket, always with you

After payment the ticket lands in Tickets, with the QR code ready for the driver, even offline.

Follow the bus live

From the ticket you can see where the bus is, the next stops and whether it is on time.
