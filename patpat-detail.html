PatPatcase study

An AI facilitator for agencies and their clients. PatPat puts scattered ideas in order, steps in when the team is stuck and keeps a living summary of every project: for when you need clarity, and a pat on the back.

01 — The brief

Giving shape to strategic thinking

The brief from TONIDIGRIGIO asked for an AI agent facilitator: something that helps the team structure strategic thinking and supports the client when it’s time to define, compare and decide, keeping the dialogue between people going through the most delicate phases of a project.

40%

of clients can’t structure their own needs

45%

19%

45%

of teams don’t reuse insights, for lack of time

Methodology

The whole challenge ran in one week: the brief arrived on Monday afternoon and on Friday PatPat was pitched to the agency. Four and a half days to go from research to a first working UI.

02 — Research

Where the work gets stuck

Talking with agency teams and clients, the same problems kept coming back. Not a lack of ideas, but too little time to think, needs that are hard to put into words and insights that get lost along the way.

Voices from the interviews

The projects that work best are the ones where we took the time to really think. The problem is it rarely happens: we’re almost always in a rush.
The real challenge isn’t being creative with solutions, it’s pinning down exactly what the real problem is.
I have lots of data, many hypotheses and growing priorities, but I struggle to turn it all into a clear strategy shared with the agency.

Four pain points

1

Needs that stay vague. Clients struggle to turn what they need into structured requests.

2

Misaligned from day one. Teams start projects without a shared view of the goals.

3

Lost insights. Good ideas from meetings are not considered or reused later.

4

Hard decisions. Both sides need support when it’s time to choose and commit.

Where a facilitator can help

Strategy

Helps plan and define measurable goals.

Relationship

Facilitates the dialogue between the client and the agency’s creative team.

Control

Flags risks, unverified assumptions and inconsistencies before a call or a decision.

Memory

Retrieves insights and learns from everything collected along the project.

What it gives each side

For the client

  • Explains ideas for the project more clearly
  • Supports communication with the team
  • Keeps track of how the work is progressing
  • Makes it easier to raise doubts along the way

For the agency team

  • Helps share progress with the client
  • Considers every insight, discarded ones too
  • Eases internal decisions
  • Only clear, meaningful requests reach the team

03 — The idea

Meet PatPat

Three needs came out of the research: people want to feel understood, get concrete help and feel able to face what worries them. PatPat is an AI assistant that loves tidying up messy ideas, so people can make more conscious decisions and work better with their team and partners.

  • Guided brainstorming that brings new ideas to the surface
  • Clarify goals, doubts and priorities before talking to the team
  • Express ideas freely, even informally or by voice
  • Turn scattered thoughts into clear, consistent and usable directions
For when you need clarity
(and a pat on the back).

Brand identity

The name is a pat on the back, and the character is literally a hand: friendly, a little clumsy, always there. The language is clean, direct and reassuring. The moodboard collects playful characters, hands and bold colours that bring order, simplicity and a touch of warmth.

05 — Process

From wireframes to a calm UI

The screens went through three stages: grey wireframes to define structure and the split between team and client, a first visual version and a final redesign that keeps the brand but quiets the interface.

Wireframes

First version

The first UI gave each screen a different saturated sidebar, on top of a colourful desktop wallpaper. It was lively, but the colour competed with the content and didn’t tell users anything.

The redesign: colour with a meaning

The final UI keeps the playful palette of the brand, but gives each colour a job. A calm, neutral base lets the content breathe; pink always means PatPat, so you see at a glance when the assistant is speaking; green and violet tell the two spaces apart, so employees and clients always know where they are.

Calm base

A light, neutral ground, just tinted for each space.
