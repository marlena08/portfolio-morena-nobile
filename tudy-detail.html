01 — The problem

Too much, or too little

For quick, everyday tasks the usual tools miss the mark. Productivity workspaces are built to plan everything, long term included, and end up heavy for a simple to-do. Notes apps are the opposite: quick, but too basic, with no priorities, progress or reminders. tudy sits in between: only what needs doing now, fast to add and easy to tick off.

What’s already out there

Too much

Notion · TickTick · Todoist

Powerful and flexible, but feature-rich interfaces and a learning curve that feel like overkill for a quick daily list.

Close, but limited

Microsoft To Do · Apple Reminders

Simple and focused, with a view for the day, but little room to organise and, for Reminders, tied to Apple devices.

Too little

Apple Notes · Google Keep

Fast to write in, but a list stays a list: no priorities, no progress and nothing that brings the right task back at the right time.

02 — The app

Screen by screen

Four tabs, Today, Tasks, Notes and Profile, plus the task detail and a short onboarding. Every screen answers one question: what do I have to do, and what’s next?

Today

The day at a glance: a week or month calendar, overdue tasks and today’s focus, sorted by priority and time, with a progress bar that fills as you go.

Tasks & history

Every task in one place, grouped by when it’s due, and a history of what’s been done. A search helps find anything quickly.

Quick notes

A space for ideas on the fly: notes can be pinned, edited or turned into a task with one tap.

Task detail

Each task can be broken into subtasks, with a progress indicator and a quick way to mark it as done.

Profile & alerts

Account, language and light or dark theme, plus the reminders: morning and evening recaps, alerts before a deadline and quiet hours.

Access & onboarding

Sign-up, login and password recovery, with a short guided tour on first use.

03 — Responsive

One app, two postures

On the phone tudy is quick and thumb-friendly, with a bottom bar and a floating “+” to add a task. On a larger screen it spreads out: a side menu and more room for the day, without changing how it works.

05 — How it was built

From design to a working app

tudy was designed and built end to end, from the first screens to a live app.

  1. Design

    Flows and screens for the main journeys, in light and dark.

  2. Build

    One web app for phone and computer, installable from the browser.

  3. Sync

    Works offline, stays up to date and in step on every device.

  4. Remind

    Alerts at the right time, respecting quiet hours.
