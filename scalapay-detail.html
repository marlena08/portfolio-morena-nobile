scalapaycase study

Two visuals for a B2C campaign brief for Scalapay: a Valentine’s Day carousel and a new key visual, both built to catch the eye and explain paying in instalments in a clear and creative way.

Role
Graphic designer
Project
B2C campaign, creative brief
Format
Social carousel & key visual
Deliverables
6 slides, 3-frame visual

01 — The brief

Paying in instalments, made visual

The project started from a creative brief for a B2C campaign. The work explores different levels of creative freedom, with one goal: visually appealing content able to grab attention and communicate the service in a clear and creative way.

Task 01

Valentine’s Day carousel

Gift ideas told as love stories, each one payable in instalments.

↓ Task 02

Key visual

A fairy-tale scene that turns one receipt into three small payments.

↓

02 — Task 01

Love at first instalment

A six-slide Instagram carousel for Valentine’s Day. It tells one love story in four chapters, from the first meeting to growing old together. A red string runs across every slide: each chapter hangs one polaroid on it, with the gift from a partner shop shown big below the photo and a price tag with the instalment, so the product is always clear without covering the story.

03 — Task 02

The magic of paying in instalments

In the new key visual the protagonist is a fairy. With a touch of her wand she turns one receipt into three parts, making the idea of paying in instalments instantly visible. A fairy-tale look and an evocative copy tell how simple and convenient the service is: it looks like magic… but it’s real.
